## Rounds

### Round 1 (2026-11-01): 1 finding, 1 confirmed

- R1-1 confirmed (low): `open()` saved the new tab to the registry before
  adding it to the in-memory map, so archiving or deleting the owning task or
  project during that write missed it in `closeForOwner`; the open then went
  on to create a host view and left a saved tab for a dead owner until the
  next backend restart. The tab is registered before the write now, and the
  open stops with "Browser tab not found" when cleanup removed it.
